# WorkOutApp

Mobile application with workout programs that require no equipment.

The app has a light **concrete basement themed gamification** to motivate users to train regularly at home.

The exercises presented here are effective and based on bodyweight training.

The application will also include basic functional exercises used in everyday life.

The basic idea is to give home/street workout to people with tips to not to harm ourselves.

Nobody of us is workout coach or dietologist, so we will not include dangerous exercises or diet tips

The exercises and tips are simple and available public so everybody can check it 

Some media files are generated with AI

## Features
- Bodyweight workout programs
- Exercise progression levels
- Training timer
- Workout history
- Gamified motivation system
- Health and Safety tips
